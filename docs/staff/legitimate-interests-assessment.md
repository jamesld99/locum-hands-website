# Legitimate interests assessment: routine Locum Hands enquiries

Approved for Locum Hands Ltd on 13 November 2026, and updated when the forms changed to inbox-only delivery. Liset Hernandez handles enquiries and is responsible for retention and deletion. This note is for staff. It is not a page on the website.

The public privacy notice states the outcome. This assessment records why.

## What this covers

Routine enquiries emailed by the Professionals, Practices and Contact forms to `hello@locumhands.co.uk`.

The inbox message is the enquiry. The forms do not save a separate database copy. The existing Neon database is left as it is, and this process does not read, change or delete it.

Contract applies instead of this assessment where using the details is necessary for a contract with the individual or for steps they ask Locum Hands to take before a contract.

## Purpose

Locum Hands receives the details a person chooses to submit, keeps them as the inbox email, and uses them to reply. The person's email address is the reply-to address. A reference is shown only after Resend accepts the send. A later attempt uses the same reference so an accepted email is not sent twice.

The form asks only for the details needed to answer. It does not ask for an account, documents or medical information. The raw IP address is not stored. A short-lived hash is kept in server memory to limit repeated submissions.

## Legitimate interests

Locum Hands' interest is answering people who ask about locum work or temporary cover, and keeping the email so a reply and a later correction or deletion request can be matched to it.

## Necessity

The name, contact details and the message are needed to reply. The inbox email is the record. A shorter record would not be enough to respond.

## Balancing

People choose to send the form. The checkbox records that they have read the privacy notice. It is not a consent decision.

Routine access to the inbox is limited to Liset Hernandez. James Lloyd-Davies may help with an enquiry in Outlook only when Liset specifically authorises it and there is a business need. James does not need database access for routine enquiry handling.

A closed routine enquiry is kept in the inbox for up to six months, and deleted sooner when it is no longer needed. Someone can email `hello@locumhands.co.uk` to ask how an enquiry is being handled, or to ask for it to be corrected or deleted.

The interest in answering the enquiry is not overridden by the person's interests or rights, given the limited details, the limited access and the retention limit.

## Contract

Use contract where using the details is necessary for a contract with the individual or for steps they ask Locum Hands to take before a contract. An active registration or booking is an example. Those records stay under the retention rule that applies to them.
