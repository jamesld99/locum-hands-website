# Monthly enquiry review for Liset Hernandez

Liset handles Locum Hands enquiries and is responsible for retention and deletion. New enquiries exist only as emails in `hello@locumhands.co.uk`. James Lloyd-Davies does not need database access for this review. He may help in Outlook only when Liset specifically authorises it and there is a business need.

Do not delete emails in bulk. Leave the existing Neon database alone: do not open it, change it or delete rows from it as part of this review.

## When an enquiry is closed

An enquiry is closed when Liset has finished dealing with it. Record the closure in a private note that only she uses.

For each enquiry she closes, record:

- the reference, which is the id in the email footer
- the date it was closed
- whether it must be kept because it is an active registration, an active booking, or a record needed for a legal obligation or a dispute

## What to delete, and when

Review the note once a month.

For a closed routine enquiry, delete the Outlook message when the information is no longer needed. Do not keep it for more than six months after the date it was closed.

Record the date the message was removed. Delete only the message whose reference is written in the note.

Leave active registrations, bookings, and records needed for a legal obligation or a dispute in place. Keep those for the period that applies to that kind of record. This review does not decide those other periods.
